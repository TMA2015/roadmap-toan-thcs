## 1. Chapter structure

### Bài 1 — Khái niệm phương trình và hệ hai phương trình bậc nhất hai ẩn
Starts printed p.5.

Observed curriculum content:
- linear equation in two variables in form `ax + by = c`, with coefficients not simultaneously zero;
- ordered-pair solution concept;
- geometric representation of the solution set as a line;
- system of two linear equations in two variables;
- system solution as a common solution of both equations;
- graphical interpretation through intersection of the two represented lines;
- recognition/checking exercises and a simple modeling setup from the opening situation.

### Bài 2 — Giải hệ hai phương trình bậc nhất hai ẩn
Starts printed p.11.

Observed curriculum content:
- substitution method;
- elimination method;
- worked examples for both;
- practice where equations need suitable manipulation before elimination;
- use of handheld calculator to find solutions of systems;
- mixed practice after the two methods.

### Luyện tập chung
Printed p.19 onward.

Observed roles:
- solving systems;
- using graphs/solution interpretation;
- consolidating multiple methods;
- mixed direct practice.

### Bài 3 — Giải bài toán bằng cách lập hệ phương trình
Starts printed p.21.

Observed curriculum workflow:
1. choose unknowns and set appropriate conditions;
2. express unknown/known quantities;
3. establish the system from relationships;
4. solve the system;
5. check conditions and state the answer.

Observed contexts/examples:
- number/division structure;
- motion;
- work/productivity;
- mixture/material-density style opening problem;
- later exercises with digits, agriculture/production, circular motion and VAT/purchase context.

### Bài tập cuối chương I
Starts printed p.24.

Observed roles:
- multiple choice checking solution/graph/solution count;
- direct solution of systems with decimals/fractions;
- systems requiring algebraic preprocessing;
- extended word problems;
- mixed end-of-chapter transfer.

## 3. Important boundary observations

### 3.1. Parameter problems are not visibly part of the reviewed SGK Chapter I core sequence
The reviewed pp.5–25 do not establish a standalone parameter-system unit comparable to the public site's current parameter section.

Therefore:
- current website `tham-so-he` must **not** be justified as KNTT-Core from this SGK chapter alone;
- keep it outside Core unless another S1 source explicitly supports it;
- current Entrance10 placement is provisionally consistent with the S1 evidence reviewed so far.

### 3.2. Method choice is pedagogically useful but needs careful wording
The SGK teaches substitution and elimination as the two main methods and supplies mixed practice. A formal standalone “choose the optimal method” skill is not visibly stated as a separate curriculum requirement in the reviewed chapter.

Therefore:
- method choice may remain a high-value pedagogical objective;
- do not overstate it as an independently mandated S1 standard without further source support.

### 3.3. Number-of-solutions depth needs separate audit
The end-of-chapter exercise set visibly includes solution-count outcomes, including no-solution/multiple-solution possibilities.

However:
- the exact required conceptual depth of “classify one/no/infinitely many solutions” should be checked against the full lesson progression and SBT/S2;
- advanced determinant-style criteria are not established as S1 Core by the pages reviewed.

### 3.4. Calculator support is present in SGK
Calculator use appears explicitly in Bài 2.

This supports:
- calculator as a checking/solving aid in the learning material;
- but calculator use should not replace understanding substitution/elimination.
