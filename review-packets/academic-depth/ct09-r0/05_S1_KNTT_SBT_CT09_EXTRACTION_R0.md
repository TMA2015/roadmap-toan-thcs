## 2. Bài 1 — concept / recognition / representation

Observed roles in the workbook:

- identify/recall the form of a linear equation in two variables;
- identify a system of two linear equations in two variables;
- determine whether an ordered pair is a solution of an equation/system;
- write/represent solution sets graphically;
- use line intersection to reason about system solutions;
- build a linear equation/system from a simple real-world context;
- simple parameter-coefficient tasks tied to whether a given pair satisfies an equation/system.

Important observation:
- SBT explicitly includes limited coefficient/parameter exercises attached to equation/system conditions.
- These do **not by themselves** justify the full public-site parameter unit as KNTT-Core; their role is narrower and must be separated from advanced Entrance10 parameter families.

## 3. Bài 2 — solving systems

Observed roles:

- solve by substitution;
- solve by elimination;
- systems with coefficients requiring multiplication/rearrangement;
- systems with decimals, fractions and irrational coefficients;
- use handheld calculator for system solutions;
- determine one / no / infinitely many solutions through solving or transformed systems;
- recover a line equation from two points via a system;

Important progression signal:

## 4. Bài 3 — modeling with systems

The workbook states the standard workflow:

1. choose unknowns and suitable conditions;
2. express unknown and known quantities;
3. establish the system from relationships;
4. solve;
5. check conditions and conclude.

Observed contexts include:

- mixture/concentration;
- break-even / cost-revenue;
- counts and classification;
- upstream/downstream motion;
- work/calorie scheduling;
- ticket/event revenue;
- book purchase/budget;
- airplane wind-speed model;
- arithmetic-sequence / distance accumulation;
- and other two-unknown real-world models.

This is stronger evidence than a narrow “word-problem” label: official S1 exercise progression expects transfer across multiple contextual structures.

## 6. S1-supported CT09 problem-type candidates after SGK + SBT

The following now have support from both SGK and/or SBT and may enter the normalized CT09 catalogue.

### A. Concept / representation

1. Recognize a linear equation in two variables.
2. Check whether an ordered pair solves a two-variable equation.
3. Represent/interpret the solution set graphically.
4. Recognize a system of two linear equations in two variables.
5. Check whether an ordered pair solves a system.
6. Interpret a system solution as line intersection/common point.
7. Use graphical structure to reason about one/no/infinitely many solutions.

### B. Direct system solving

8. Solve by substitution.
9. Solve by elimination.
10. Transform equations before applying the method.
11. Solve systems with fractional/decimal coefficients.
12. Solve systems with irrational coefficients where still linear.
13. Use calculator support to solve/check a system.
14. Distinguish unique / no / infinitely many solutions from the solving process.

### C. Coefficient / parameter-lite Core practice

15. Find coefficients so a given ordered pair satisfies an equation/system.
16. Solve a system after substituting a specified parameter value.
17. Use system conditions to recover a line/equation or constrained coefficient.

Boundary:
- these are **S1-supported narrow coefficient/parameter applications**;
- they must not be conflated with advanced free-parameter classification/optimization problems typical of Entrance10/Challenge.

### D. Modeling / application

18. Set up and solve a system from a count/value/revenue problem.
19. Number/digit problem.
20. Motion / upstream-downstream problem.
21. Work/productivity/scheduling problem.
22. Mixture/concentration/composition problem.
23. Cost-revenue / break-even problem.
24. Purchase/budget problem.
25. Rate/wind-speed or relative-speed model.
26. Nutrition/fuel/resource composition problem.
27. Sequence/distance accumulation or other structured two-unknown context.

The later S2/S3 audit may merge some of 18–27 into broader canonical modeling families.

## 7. Immediate audit implications

### 7.1. Interactive-bank quantity is not enough
The public CT09 Practice Bank may contain 120 questions, but SBT shows several distinct roles that must be checked separately:
- graphical meaning;
- solution-count structure;
- coefficient/parameter-lite tasks;
- richer modeling contexts;
- direct vs transfer problems.

The next audit must measure **problem-type coverage**, not question count.

### 7.2. Written work deserves heavier weight
Bài 3 and the end-of-chapter review contain multiple contexts where:
- choosing unknowns;
- setting conditions;
- forming equations;
- checking the result

are essential parts of the solution.

Therefore MCQ-only evidence cannot represent the full learning objective for this section.

### 7.3. Parameter boundary must be refined, not simply removed
SGK alone did not visibly justify a broad parameter unit as Core. SBT now shows limited parameter/coefficient tasks inside official practice.

Thus the final catalogue should separate:
- **Core parameter-lite / coefficient constraints** that are directly present in S1;
- **Entrance10 advanced parameter families** that require additional S2/S3 evidence.
