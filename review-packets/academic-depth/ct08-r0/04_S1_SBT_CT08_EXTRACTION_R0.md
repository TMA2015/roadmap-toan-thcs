## 3. Bài 6 — first-degree inequalities

SBT Bài 6 exercises 2.14–2.19 include several distinct structures.

### SBT-INEQ-01 — direct first-degree inequalities
Bài 2.14:
- positive and negative coefficients;
- strict and non-strict inequality signs;
- sign reversal is required for negative coefficient cases.

### SBT-INEQ-02 — transform before solving
Bài 2.15:
- unknowns on both sides;
- cancellation of higher-degree-looking terms can reduce the task to a first-degree inequality.

Bài 2.16:
- products are expanded/reduced;
- apparent quadratic terms cancel, leaving a first-degree inequality.

This means a self-study learner should not see only already-normalized `ax+b > 0` forms.

### SBT-INEQ-03 — maximum-load modeling with discrete conclusion
Bài 2.17:
- maximum elevator load;
- form a first-degree inequality;
- solve a continuous bound;
- then interpret a number-of-people variable as a natural number and take the largest valid integer.

This is a meaningful modeling/condition-check structure.

### SBT-INEQ-04 — minimum-earnings modeling
Bài 2.18:
- hourly rate × hours;
- “at least” condition;
- form and solve an inequality;
- contextual minimum conclusion.

### SBT-INEQ-05 — geometry/feasibility interval
Bài 2.19:
- a rectangle dimension relation gives one inequality;
- positivity of a length gives another condition;
- combine both to obtain an interval.

This supports the teaching value of intersecting multiple conditions, but it does **not by itself require** a standalone learner-facing “system of inequalities” family at the same status as the Bài 6 Core concept. The later catalogue should distinguish **condition intersection as support/transfer** from a separate Core family if appropriate.
