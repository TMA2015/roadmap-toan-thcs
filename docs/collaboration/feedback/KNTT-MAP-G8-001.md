## Corrections

### 1. Grade 8 factorization Core

Do **not** classify `tach-hang-tu-giua` as KNTT Grade 8 Core.

Core Bài 9 should center on:
- common factor;
- identities;
- grouping;
- combinations of those methods.

The bank may keep `tach-hang-tu-giua` as extension.

### 2. Grade 8 equation scope

Grade 8 Core:
- equation concept/solution;
- first-degree equation;
- transformations to ax+b=0;
- word problems by forming an equation.

Do not expose as Grade 8 Core:
- product equations;
- equations with variable denominators;
- first-degree inequalities;
- parameter problems.

### 3. Probability language

Prefer the KNTT Grade 8 language:
- possible outcomes;
- favorable outcomes;
- probability by ratio;
- experimental probability.

Do not require formal `khong-gian-mau` terminology as the core label when the textbook does not make it the primary wording.

### 4. Pythagoras dependency

Similarity can provide one proof/connection, but `dong-dang-gg` is **not a mandatory prerequisite** for Pythagoras.

Prefer:
`square + square root + right triangle → Pythagoras → right-triangle metric/trigonometry`

### 5. Mastery policy

Do not recommend an absolute hard gate such as 100% mastery before unlocking later material.

Project policy:
- soft mastery;
- prerequisite warning;
- targeted remediation;
- continued access;
- mastery estimated from repeated accuracy + hint usage + history.

Avoid language such as “mất gốc vĩnh viễn”. The Roadmap is explicitly designed so gaps can be traced and repaired.
