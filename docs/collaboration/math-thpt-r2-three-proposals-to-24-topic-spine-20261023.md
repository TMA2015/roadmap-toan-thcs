## 4. Source-specific findings

### Gemini 20 — strengths and defects

- KEEP: four accessible navigation groups, concise topic labels, a Grade11 logarithm learning example with explicit conceptual definition, Base→Apply questions, progressive hints and authentic written modelling.
- MERGE: split broad Oxy vs vector material (Gemini12) into TH13–TH14; merge redundant topic-level subdivisions in old 31; consolidate statistics ungrouped vs grouped as TH20–TH21 with internal subskills.
- **REJECT_FROM_KNTT_CORE: Gemini06 "Số phức lớp 12".** The owner-provided SGK KNTT Grade12 volumes list chapters I–VI on derivatives, space vectors, grouped dispersion, integrals, Oxyz geometry, conditional probability — **no Complex Numbers chapter**. Keep as optional math enrichment only if justified by separate sources; avoid old-programme leakage.
- **HOLD:** claim that some advanced radical/absolute-value equations, general systems and solid-of-revolution material are all *Core* in the stated year without exact SGK programme placement. Correct later per concept.
- **CORRECT:** examples with logarithmic product/quotient laws require positive arguments; change-of-base requires valid positive base !=1 and argument >0. A real logarithm of a power requires domain check rather than an unconditional rule for arbitrary real x/alpha.
- **CORRECT:** for Richter-like examples distinguish `log10(I/I0)` from `log10(I)` unless I is explicitly defined as dimensionless normalized intensity; do not imply real seismic formula is always the simplified one.
- **CORE UX:** avoid an additional long question bank or written duplicate inside Core Card; use short Micro in Core and canonical links to Practice and Written. No Gemini paid API integration required; static help and optional external tutor are separate policy decisions.
- **TIME:** per-card 30-minute estimate is a candidate, not a default; full session may be ~30 minutes, Core explanation shorter.

### Google.txt 25 — strengths and defects

- KEEP: distinction between classical spatial incidence/parallelism, perpendicularity/metric, Oxy/Oxyz, source-aware written-anchor reasoning graphs; rubrics modelling→conditions→method→calculation→conclusion.
- MERGE: Google01/02 quadratic functions; Google06/07 trigonometry; Google09–10 keep separation of limits vs derivative/graph survey; Google14/15 Oxy+Conic as nested families under TH14; Google24/25 statistics under TH20–21 by sampled vs grouped skill distinctions.
- **CORRECT:** Google sample puts Grade10 quadratic `Chương III` for KNTT; the **provided Grade10 KNTT SGK Volume 2 contents** show **Chương VI, Bài 16**. KNTT placement must not borrow chapter numbering from Cánh Diều/CTST without source proof.
- **HOLD:** unsourced claims like SAT quadratic share "~15% of Algebra questions", topic-specific frequency rates, “100% overlap”, fixed SAT trig 5–10%, grade-local 4-param graph translations, complex-number exam claims; require official test blueprint and item corpus or drop.
- **MODIFY:** Thinking Flow is a written Anchor artifact and compact optional teacher help, not a mandatory heavyweight per-item flowchart. A student's self-checked rubric is never independent mastery evidence.
- **PROGRAMME:** subjects such as general mathematical induction, advanced systems, angle subtleties should be mapped to verified S1 scope or OPTIONAL before publishing.

## 5. International-exam bridge (verified official blueprint, no new Core)

- Current **Digital SAT** official domains: **Algebra / Advanced Math / Problem-Solving and Data Analysis / Geometry and Trigonometry**; direct College Board source: https://satsuite.collegeboard.org/sat/whats-on-the-test/math/overview and https://satsuite.collegeboard.org/k12-educators/about/alignment/math .
- SAT emphasizes linear systems/inequalities; nonlinear **quadratic/exponential** functions; ratios, rates, proportions and percentages; distributions/two-way data, statistical claims; basic geometry/trigonometry. Do not label full **logarithm procedural mastery** as a Digital SAT core skill just because logarithms are adjacent to exponential growth.
- **ACT enhanced** official 2026 blueprint: Math **45 MCQ / 50 min**, including Number & Quantity, Algebra, Functions, Geometry, Statistics & Probability; modelling/integrated skills also important; four answer choices. Source https://www.act.org/content/act/en/products-and-services/the-act/test-preparation/act-exam-sections-and-structure.html . This differs from SAT's adaptive and student-produced response workflow; **do not** call ACT an identical four-choice/short-answer SAT exam.
- **Calculus/integrals** remain essential for Vietnam THPT KNTT but **not** Digital SAT core, nor enhanced ACT Math test syllabus; label foreign-exam relevance **NO_DIRECT_EXAM_SCOPE** rather than SAT priority.
- **SAT/ACT bridge overlay**: versioned `exam_alignment` and `language_of_item`/English mathematical vocabulary per canonical skill; **no extra mandatory cross-grade topics**. Add separate OPTIONAL practice for English word problems, unit rates, percent, scatterplots, inference/design of studies, technology/Desmos, calculator policy, pace and strategy; these are not automatically covered by THPT KNTT topic names.
- “Knowing all 24 topics” `!=` “SAT/ACT ready”; independent exam-domain diagnostic with officially aligned items is needed.

## 6. Golden Learn / Practice / Readiness / Written rules

- Learning Core Card: concepts, conditions, 1–2 worked reasonings, common traps, brief Micro with hint. Not another 10–20 item practice bank.
- Practice: structured subskill coverage, diagnostic answer feedback, transfer/mixed methods, optional English word problems.
- Readiness: independent; no hint/tutor, no immediate reveal, final-submit-only feedback; evidence separate.
- Written: one canonical item identity; progressive nonspoiling hints where needed, full solution, WHY, common errors, rubric, Thinking Flow for **selected** Deep Anchors.
- Scoring: do not count self-checked paper as proof of Mastery/Readiness. Maintain separate THCS vs THPT `learner_evidence_namespace`.
- Optional SAT/ACT labels are versioned `exam_alignment`; never mix exam domain, curriculum layer, difficulty, or learner mastery.
