## 2. Coverage blueprint (CANDIDATE, not externally reviewed)

- **B10 D1:** basic incidence axioms of point/line/plane; three ways to determine a plane, intersection line of two planes and point of intersection of a line with plane; plane sections of tetrahedra/pyramids; avoid assuming arbitrary perspective lines physically intersect.
- **B11 D1:** coplanar distinct parallel lines versus skew lines; criteria for parallel lines in 3D and consequences of parallels in two intersecting planes; illustrative counterexamples to false transitivity assumptions where hypotheses are missing.
- **B12 D1:** line parallel to plane definition versus line lying in plane; standard test using a contained parallel line under plane-exclusion hypothesis; intersection of planes containing a line parallel to another plane; construct sections with legitimate intersection points.
- **B13 D1:** parallel planes, tests and basic consequences, intersections of a third plane yielding parallel lines when hypotheses hold; 3D configurations and section geometry; avoid asserting planes parallel merely because one pair of contained lines is parallel.
- **B14 D1:** projection along a direction not parallel to the target plane, straight lines mapped to lines or points depending on projection direction; preserves collinearity, parallelism in non-degenerate cases, ratios of directed segments on same line; oblique projected lengths/angles **not** generally preserved.
- **Cross-lesson RD0:** distinguish (a\parallel b), (a\parallel (P)), ((P)\parallel(Q)) and skew lines; one coherent construction/diagram-based proof with explicit hypotheses, not five separate unrelated demo pages.
