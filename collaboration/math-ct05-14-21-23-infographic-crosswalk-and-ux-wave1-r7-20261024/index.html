    3. New original CT05 mathematical diagram: academic caveat

    docs/assets/infographics/05/05-05-mo-hinh-dien-tich-binh-phuong-r2.svg - Diagram divides a square of side (a+b) into (a\times a), two (a\times b), and (b\times b) areas. - Graphical construction is defined for (a>0,b>0); its area reasoning shows ((a+b)^2=a^2+ab+ab+b^2=a^2+2ab+b^2). - The algebraic identity is valid for all real (a,b), regardless of whether a drawn rectangle has positive side lengths. - Diagram role is concept_visual, not text_recap; includes SVG title and desc for screen-reader/semantic source review. - Review still needed: confirm the displayed geometry/sizes/labels, legibility and area illustration on physical iPad and small screens; do not claim independent NotebookLM PASS. Do not delete the original seven-formula explanation.
